Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocuNexa PDF Editor & Maker (“DocuNexa,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and documents

DocuNexa is designed to process documents on your device. PDF files, images, and other content you choose to open or work with are handled by the app to provide its features. Do not use the app for documents you are not authorized to access.

The app is intended to work without requiring you to create an account, sign in, or provide a phone number. The app does not intentionally upload your document contents to our servers as part of its ordinary on-device document workflow.

2. Data collection and tracking

DocuNexa is designed not to collect personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not require an account. If a future version adds analytics, advertising, cloud processing, or other third-party services, this policy should be updated to explain those services and the information they process before they are enabled.

3. Files and device permissions

When you select a document, the app uses Android’s system file picker or the relevant device functionality to let you choose files. The app may keep a list of recently opened document entries so you can access them from within the app. You can use the app’s “Clear Recent History” option to clear those entries, where available. This does not necessarily delete the original files from your device.

4. Storage and security

Document processing is intended to take place locally on your device. Your device’s security, screen lock, operating-system updates, and file-sharing choices also affect the safety of your information. No method of storage or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-party services

This policy describes DocuNexa’s intended core, on-device functionality. If the version you use includes links to external websites or integrates third-party services, those services may process information under their own privacy policies. Please review their policies before using them.

6. Children’s privacy

DocuNexa is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through an account-registration process.

7. Your choices

  • You can choose which files to open using the system file picker.
  • You can stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.
  • You can clear recent-document entries using the in-app option, where available.
  • Uninstalling the app may remove app-specific local data, but it does not necessarily delete documents saved elsewhere on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact
